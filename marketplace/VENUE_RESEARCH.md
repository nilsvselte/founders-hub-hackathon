## Photo review and catalog decision

Migration `019_verified_remaining_venues.sql` publishes the seven venues with confirmed meeting/event offerings above, plus **Regus Powerhouse**, whose operator is now confirmed. A venue's own exterior or shared-space photograph is sufficient for the demo when labelled **Venue photo**; it does not establish an individual room's appearance or specifications.

Seven venues now use their own meeting/event-space images: Clarion, Britannia, Prinsen, Panorama, Comfort Trondheim, Grand Olav and Work-Work. The local images replace facades, lounge or bar photos. Regus retains its verified Powerhouse building image, labelled as a venue photo. The named room shown in a photo does not identify every generic sample room at that venue.

The migration also replaces the Spaces display name with Regus Powerhouse while preserving stable internal IDs and booking prefixes, corrects Grand Olav's unsupported single-room claim, and removes Clarion's inconsistent room-count claim. Host-owned listings and customized descriptions are preserved. BargaFlyt, Comfort Park, Thon Nidaros and Augustin remain drafts.
